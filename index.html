Aamir Shehzad

Department of Philosophy • National and Kapodistrian University of Athens

I am a researcher at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (Department of Philosophy), in the field of Political Philosophy. My research interests span across Homer, Hannah Arendt, Simone Weil, Aristotle, and Eastern Orthodox philosophy/theology. I am also a co-host at the TV Broadcast Philosophical Dialogues (DION TV – Greece) and Research Lead for the Aigis Institute.

I have published across diverse academic platforms – from Political Theory to Intellectual History – emphasising republican theory, democratic political movements, and political violence. At the same time, I am actively working with cultural and educational diplomacy projects, promoting cross-cultural dialogue through communication with diplomats and religious figures.
