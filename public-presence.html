Television & Media

Media Presence

Aamir Shehzad is co-host of Philosophical Dialogues on DION TV (Greece), bringing academic philosophy to a broader audience through televised discussions on political philosophy, ethics, and the history of ideas. He also engages with the broader public through media interviews, essays, and commentary on topics at the intersection of ancient philosophy and contemporary political life.
