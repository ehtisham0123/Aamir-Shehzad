Work in Progress

Edited Volumes & Companions

● Work in Progress Edited Volume

Perspectives on Just War theory (WIP) – Edited Volume

A multi-author edited volume bringing together philosophical perspectives on just war theory, from classical antiquity and Byzantine political theology through to modern international law and applied ethics.

● Work in Progress Edited Volume

Ethical Leadership (WIP) – Edited Volume

An edited volume examining the moral and philosophical dimensions of political leadership across historical periods, from Platonic virtue ethics to contemporary leadership theory.

● Work in Progress Brill Edited Companion

Brill’s Companion to Simone Weil’s Reception of Classical Antiquity (WIP) – Edited Companion

An edited companion for Brill Academic Publishers examining how Simone Weil engaged with and reinterpreted the intellectual heritage of ancient Greece throughout her philosophical writing.

Journal

Dia-noesis: A Journal of Philosophy

Aamir Shehzad serves as co-editor of Dia-noesis, a peer-reviewed journal of philosophy published by the National Documentation Centre of Greece (EKT).
