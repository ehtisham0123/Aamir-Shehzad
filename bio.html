Academic Profile

Aamir Shehzad is a researcher at the Department of Philosophy, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, in the field of Political Philosophy. His research interests span across Homer, Hannah Arendt, Simone Weil, Aristotle, and Eastern Orthodox philosophy/theology.

He is co-host of the TV broadcast Philosophical Dialogues on DION TV (Greece), and serves as Research Lead for the Aigis Institute.

Research Interests

  • Political Theory and Intellectual History
  • Republican theory and democratic political movements
  • Homer, Hannah Arendt, Simone Weil, Aristotle
  • Eastern Orthodox philosophy and theology
  • Just War theory and political violence
  • Cultural and educational diplomacy
  • Byzantine political philosophy and imperial legitimacy

Publications

Aamir Shehzad has published across diverse academic platforms – from Political Theory to Intellectual History – emphasising republican theory, democratic political movements, and political violence. His monograph Ancient Greek Democracy and American Republicanism was published in 2025.

He is co-editor of Dia-noesis: A Journal of Philosophy, published by the National Documentation Centre of Greece. For a complete list of publications, visit his Academia.edu profile.

Teaching & Academic Positions

In 2026, Aamir Shehzad held a Visiting Professorship at the Università degli Studi di Macerata, Italy. Since 2022, he has directed the short course “Ancient Perspectives — Modern Challenges” in Greece. From 2021 to 2022, he participated in international development and educational diplomacy initiatives through the Kurdistan Regional Institute (KRI).

Public Engagement

Aamir Shehzad is actively working with cultural and educational diplomacy projects, promoting cross-cultural dialogue through communication with diplomats and religious figures. He co-hosts Philosophical Dialogues on DION TV (Greece), bringing academic philosophy to a broader audience.
